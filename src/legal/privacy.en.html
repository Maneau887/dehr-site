This policy explains what data DEHR Ltd ("we") receives when you use dehr.ltd or write to us, and how we handle it. We follow Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 1981 (including Amendment 13), and for visitors from the European Union, the GDPR as well.

1. Who is responsible for your data

DEHR Ltd, staffing agency, Israel. Data questions: WhatsApp or phone +972 54-873-2037, Telegram @dehrcompany.

2. What we receive

  • The website itself does not collect applications or CVs. It has no forms for entering personal details.
  • When you write to us on WhatsApp or Telegram, we receive what you send: your name, phone number, messages, and any documents or photos you choose to share.
  • Analytics. Only if you click "Allow" does the site load Yandex Metrica. It collects non-identifying visit data: pages, clicks, device type, approximate region and a recording of on-page actions (Webvisor). If you decline, it is not loaded.
  • The job map is loaded from OpenFreeMap. Like any web server, it sees your device's IP address.

3. Why we use it

  • to reply to you and find a suitable job;
  • to arrange your employment if you accept a job;
  • to understand how the site is used and improve it (only with your analytics consent).

We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

An employer, only the data needed for a specific job and only after you have agreed to it. The services that carry our communication and host the site: WhatsApp (Meta), Telegram, Yandex (Metrica, with consent), OpenFreeMap, GitHub (site hosting). Authorities, when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

Conversations and candidate data are kept for as long as needed to find you work and meet legal obligations, and no longer than 3 years after the last contact unless the law requires otherwise. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

6. Your rights

You can ask at any time what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw your consent. Contact us using the details in section 1. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can contact the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority or, if you are in the EU, your local supervisory authority.

7. Cookies and consent

The site only stores your choice from the consent window in your browser. Yandex Metrica cookies are set only if you click "Allow". To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and the consent window will appear again.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
