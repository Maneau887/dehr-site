About us

How it all began.

The story of DEHR Ltd began long before it was officially registered. Its founder faced the difficulties of job hunting himself: unmet expectations, the language barrier and improper paperwork.

He saw the problem from both sides: skilled people without work, and companies urgently needing reliable staff. Helping gradually became a system, and an official company followed.
